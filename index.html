01 · The through-line

Quantitative work for consequential decisions.

Credit decisions are made twice: first when a model scores an applicant, and again when someone asks whether the policy built around that decision actually worked.

I work across both halves. My projects combine interpretable credit models, cost-sensitive thresholds, and quasi-experimental evaluation of financial-access policy. The aim is not just a precise estimate; it is a defensible answer with its assumptions in view.
